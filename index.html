Frequently asked questions

Before you send your coins

How much is my coin worth?

Coin value depends on authenticity, rarity, year, mint mark, condition, collector demand, and sometimes metal content. Photos can help with an initial assessment, but final value may require physical inspection.

Do you buy damaged or worn coins?

Sometimes. Wear and damage can affect value, but older or scarce coins may still be worth reviewing. Send photos before deciding to discard anything.

Do you buy foreign coins?

Yes, selected foreign coins and collections may be considered depending on age, rarity, condition, and demand.

Is the photo evaluation final?

No. A photo-based evaluation is only preliminary. Authenticity, weight, dimensions, metal, and other details may need to be checked in person.

How do I contact you?

Use the Facebook or Messenger buttons on this website. Include clear photos and any information you know about the coin.
